# Curated legal sources

These files contain selected statutory text extracted from official State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) republications, checked on 2026-10-27. The text was copied programmatically from the official HTML; it was not generated by an LLM. Only paragraph whitespace was normalized.

- `labor.json`: 24 articles from the Labor Contract Law (2012 amendment), Labor Law (2018 amendment), and Social Insurance Law (2018 amendment).
- `tenancy.json`: 24 articles from the Civil Code and Housing Leasing Regulation (promulgated 2025-07-16, effective 2025-09-15).
- `SOURCES.json`: official URLs, published versions, selected article numbers, source-HTML SHA-256 values, and extraction notes.

Every article includes its official URL, checked date, version, and a SHA-256 value for the extracted text. `load_laws` checks content hashes before injection. These checks detect accidental edits; they do not establish that a statute is current or applicable to a particular contract. Refreshing the library requires checking the official text and relevant amendments again.

## Scope

The user must select Chinese mainland jurisdiction before statutes are injected. Keyword classification routes recognizable Chinese labor and lease contracts; it does not determine governing law. Foreign-jurisdiction markers in the existing Illinois sample cause routing to `other`, even if mainland jurisdiction is selected. Uncertain or unsupported inputs receive text-based risk hints without statutory citations.

The library omits many statutes, local regulations, judicial interpretations, and exceptions. Housing Leasing Regulation Article 2 limits its scope to housing on urban state-owned land. Civil Code Article 712 expressly permits other contractual arrangements for maintenance duties; an unfavorable allocation must not automatically be described as unlawful. Article matching and ID membership do not establish legal applicability or whether a cited provision supports an explanation.
