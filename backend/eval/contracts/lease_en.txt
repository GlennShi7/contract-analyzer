RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made between Prairie Lane Properties LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned resident or residents ("Tenant"). Each person who signs this Lease as Tenant is a Tenant under this Lease.

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the apartment located at 1207 West Oregon Street, Unit 3B, Urbana, Illinois 61801 (the "Premises"), together with the appliances and furnishings listed on the Move-In Condition Report. The Premises shall be used only as a private residence for the persons named in this Lease.

2. TERM. The term of this Lease begins at 12:00 p.m. on August 15, 2026 and ends at 12:00 p.m. on July 31, 2027. If Landlord is unable to deliver possession on the start date because a prior occupant has not vacated, rent shall be abated on a daily basis until possession is delivered, and Landlord shall not otherwise be liable for the delay.

3. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,250.00, due in advance on or before the first day of each month, without demand or deduction. Rent shall be paid through the Landlord's online resident portal. A convenience fee charged by the payment processor for card payments shall be paid by Tenant. Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

4. LATE CHARGES AND RETURNED PAYMENTS. If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the fifth day of the month, Tenant shall pay an initial late charge of $50.00 plus $10.00 for each additional day until rent is paid in full. Any payment returned for insufficient funds shall incur a $35.00 returned payment fee, and Landlord may thereafter require Tenant to pay by certified funds.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,250.00 upon signing this Lease. The security deposit may not be used by Tenant as payment of the last month's rent. Landlord may deduct from the security deposit unpaid rent, unpaid utilities, and the cost of repairing damage beyond ordinary wear and tear. Within 45 days after Tenant vacates, Landlord shall return the security deposit, less any lawful deductions, to the forwarding address provided by Tenant in writing. If any deduction is made for damage, Landlord shall provide an itemized statement of the damage and the estimated or actual cost of repair, with paid receipts or copies of them when available.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant shall establish service in Tenant's own name and pay for electricity, gas, internet, and cable television. Landlord shall provide water, sewer, and trash collection, and the cost of these services is included in the rent. Tenant shall keep heat at no less than 55 degrees Fahrenheit at all times to prevent pipes from freezing, and Tenant shall be responsible for any damage caused by frozen pipes if the heat was turned off or set below this level.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and sanitary and shall promptly report any needed repairs through the resident portal. Landlord shall make repairs needed to keep the Premises fit to live in. Tenant shall pay the first $150.00 of the cost of each repair call, regardless of the cause, unless the repair is required by an emergency affecting health or safety. Tenant shall replace light bulbs and smoke detector batteries during the term at Tenant's expense. Tenant shall pay the full cost of repairing any damage caused by Tenant, Tenant's guests, or pets.

8. LANDLORD'S RIGHT OF ENTRY. Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at reasonable times to inspect, make repairs, or show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, after giving Tenant at least 24 hours' notice by email or a notice posted on the door. In case of emergency, Landlord may enter at any time without notice. During the last 90 days of the term, Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants on a schedule set by Landlord.

9. OCCUPANCY AND GUESTS. Only the persons named as Tenant in this Lease may live in the Premises. A guest may not stay in the Premises for more than seven consecutive nights or more than fourteen nights in any calendar month without Landlord's prior written consent. Any person staying longer shall be treated as an unauthorized occupant, and Landlord may charge Tenant an unauthorized occupant fee of $100.00 per day.

10. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT. Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without Landlord's prior written consent. Any proposed subtenant must complete Landlord's application and meet Landlord's rental criteria. If Landlord approves a sublease, Tenant shall pay a sublease processing fee of $200.00, and Tenant shall remain fully responsible for all obligations under this Lease.

11. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may terminate this Lease before the end of the term only by giving Landlord at least 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two months' rent. The early termination fee is in addition to rent due through the termination date. Tenant shall not be entitled to a refund of any rent or fees paid in advance.

12. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. This Lease shall automatically renew for an additional twelve-month term at the then-current market rent set by Landlord unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the term. Landlord shall inform Tenant of the renewal rent at least 75 days before the end of the term.

13. JOINT AND SEVERAL LIABILITY. If more than one person signs this Lease as Tenant, each Tenant is jointly and severally liable for all obligations under this Lease, including the full amount of rent and any damages, regardless of which Tenant caused the obligation. The move-out of one Tenant does not release that Tenant from this Lease.

14. PETS. No animals of any kind, including animals belonging to guests, may be kept in the Premises, even temporarily, without Landlord's prior written consent. If an unauthorized animal is found in the Premises, Tenant shall pay a fine of $500.00 per occurrence and the cost of any cleaning, de-fleaing, or repairs. This section does not apply to assistance animals for a person with a disability.

15. SMOKING. Smoking of any substance, including cigarettes, cigars, marijuana, and electronic cigarettes, is prohibited inside the Premises, on balconies, and within 25 feet of the building. Tenant shall pay a $300.00 cleaning charge for any violation of this section.

16. NOISE AND CONDUCT. Tenant shall not disturb other residents or neighbors. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. on weeknights and from midnight to 9:00 a.m. on weekends. Tenant is responsible for the conduct of Tenant's guests. A second written warning for disturbance within the term shall be a default under this Lease.

17. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, change locks, or make any alteration to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Small nail holes for hanging pictures are permitted. Any approved alteration shall become the property of Landlord unless Landlord requires Tenant to remove it and restore the Premises at Tenant's expense.

18. PARKING. One assigned parking space is available in the rear lot for $60.00 per month under a separate parking agreement. Vehicles parked without a valid permit, or in another resident's space, may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense without further notice.

19. RENTER'S INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain renter's insurance with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 for the entire term and shall name Landlord as an interested party. Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's personal property, and Landlord is not responsible for loss of or damage to Tenant's property from theft, fire, water, or any other cause not caused by Landlord's negligence.

20. KEYS AND LOCKOUTS. Tenant will receive two apartment keys and one mailbox key. Lost keys shall be replaced at a charge of $75.00 per key, which includes rekeying the lock. If Tenant is locked out, Landlord's staff will unlock the door during business hours at no charge; after business hours, a lockout fee of $85.00 shall apply.

21. DEFAULT. If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may serve a written five-day notice demanding payment. If Tenant fails to comply with any other term of this Lease, Landlord may serve a ten-day notice of termination. Landlord's acceptance of partial rent shall not waive Landlord's right to proceed with eviction unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

22. ATTORNEY'S FEES. In any legal action to enforce this Lease, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable attorney's fees and court costs, whether or not the action proceeds to judgment.

23. MOVE-OUT AND CLEANING. Tenant shall return the Premises in the same condition as at move-in, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and shall return all keys by 12:00 p.m. on the last day of the term. A professional carpet cleaning charge of $175.00 shall be deducted from the security deposit at move-out regardless of the condition of the carpet. Any personal property left in the Premises after Tenant vacates shall be considered abandoned and may be disposed of by Landlord without liability.

24. HOLDOVER. If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the term without a new written lease, Tenant shall pay rent for each day of holdover at a rate of 150 percent of the daily rent, and Tenant shall be liable for any damages Landlord suffers because the Premises are not available to a new tenant.

25. MOVE-IN CONDITION REPORT. Within five days after move-in, Tenant shall complete and return the Move-In Condition Report describing any existing damage. If Tenant does not return the report, the Premises shall be deemed to have been delivered in good condition, and Tenant may be charged for any damage found at move-out.

26. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord shall be sent through the resident portal or to the management office at 400 South Goodwin Avenue, Urbana, Illinois. Notices to Tenant may be delivered by email to the address on file, through the portal, or by posting on the door of the Premises.

27. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease, the Move-In Condition Report, and the Community Rules contain the entire agreement between the parties. Any change to this Lease must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision of this Lease is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in effect. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois and the ordinances of the City of Urbana.
